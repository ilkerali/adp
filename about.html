Who we are

A citizens' association, not a company and not a party.

The Association for Digital Progress — ADP · KODEX — is a voluntary, independent, non-partisan, non-governmental and non-profit association of citizens, established under the Constitution of the Republic of North Macedonia and the Law on Associations and Foundations.

It acquired the status of a legal entity on the day of its entry into the Central Register of the Republic of North Macedonia. Its registered seat is in Gostivar, and it may open regional offices, project offices, information centres and innovation and educational centres in the country and abroad.

Our activities create openings for individuals, institutions, researchers, educators and whole communities to benefit from technological advancement — and, just as importantly, to shape it. We work across academia, civil society, schools, public bodies and the private sector, because none of these can carry digital transformation alone.

Mission

To contribute to the development of digital society, innovation, education, science and sustainable development.

Through modern information technologies, international cooperation, research, training and the development of human capacity — working in the public interest and promoting equality, inclusion, transparency, digital ethics and European values.

Vision

A recognised platform for digital innovation, academic cooperation and project development.

Regionally rooted and European-facing: an organisation that partners can rely on for delivery, that researchers can publish with, and that a school in a small municipality can actually reach.

How we work

Six values, applied to real decisions.

Innovation

We back creative thinking and new approaches to social and educational problems, including ones that might not work.

Knowledge

Research, education and access to reliable information are the foundation of everything else we do.

Inclusion

Equal access to digital opportunity, with deliberate attention to who is usually left out.

Cooperation

Progress happens between organisations, sectors and countries — rarely inside one of them.

Responsibility

Ethical, transparent and accountable use of technology and artificial intelligence, reviewed by our own commission.

Sustainability

Solutions that still stand after the project funding ends.
