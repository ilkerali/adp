ADP Journal of
Digital Progress

An international academic platform for research on digital transformation, technology, innovation, education, artificial intelligence and related interdisciplinary fields — developed according to principles of academic integrity, editorial independence, transparency and rigorous peer review.

Scope

Artificial intelligence Digital transformation Computer science & IT Educational technology Data science Digital society Technology & law AI ethics & governance Cyber security Digital economy Digital humanities Technology policy

Editorial principles

  • Double-blind peer review
  • Editorial independence from the association's bodies
  • Declared conflicts of interest
  • Transparent authorship and AI-use disclosure
  • Open access wherever funding allows
