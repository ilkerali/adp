Digital development is a public interest, not a private advantage.

Four fields of work

Digital literacy

Training, workshops and summer schools that close the digital gap for young people, women, older citizens and people with disabilities.

Education and research

Support for education at every level, STEM programmes, applied research and innovation labs.

Responsible technology

Cybersecurity, data protection and human-centred artificial intelligence, governed by our own published policies.

International cooperation

EU and regional partnerships, project development and contribution to the UN Sustainable Development Goals.
