Why we exist

Technology only counts as progress when people can use it.

Our mission is to contribute to the development of a digital society — innovation, education, science and sustainable growth — through modern information technologies, international cooperation, research, training and the development of human capacity.

We work in the public interest and promote equality, inclusion, transparency, digital ethics and European values. That means the same attention goes to a rural school without stable connectivity as to a research lab publishing on machine learning.

Mission

Build digital capability across education, science, civil society and public institutions.

A recognised platform for digital innovation, academic cooperation and project development in the region.

What we do

Six programme areas that feed into each other.

All areas of work

Digital transformation

Helping institutions, schools and civil society organisations move to digital ways of working — responsibly and with a plan.

AI & emerging technology

Following developments in artificial intelligence and promoting its responsible use in education, research and public services.

Education & digital skills

Training, courses, summer schools and lifelong-learning programmes for students, teachers and professionals.

Research & academic work

Interdisciplinary research, studies, analyses and evaluations, published openly wherever possible.

Hackathons, competitions, start-up support and innovation forums that give young people a first real project.

Digital inclusion

Closing the digital gap for rural communities, women, older people and people with disabilities.
