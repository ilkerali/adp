Getting started

From first e-mail to signed partnership.

Deadlines in EU calls are unforgiving, so we keep the early steps short. Send the call reference and the deadline in your first message and we will come back with a yes or a no quickly.

Send a partnership request
  1. Step 1

    Tell us the call and the deadline

    Programme, action type, submission date and the role you need filled.

  2. Step 2

    Short fit check

    We check the theme against our statutory objectives and current capacity, and answer within a few working days.

  3. Step 3

    Partner documents

    We send the PIF/OID details, registration documents, ethics and GDPR policies and a mandate letter.

  4. Step 4

    Work package input

    We draft our tasks, budget lines and dissemination contribution directly into your application.

  5. Step 5

    Board decision

    The Managing Board formally approves participation and the President signs.
