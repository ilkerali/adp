Digital transformation

Supporting the effective and responsible use of digital technology in education, civil society, public institutions and community development — from digitising a municipal service to helping a small NGO get off paper.

  • Digital readiness assessments and roadmaps
  • Digital public services and e-government literacy
  • Smart city and smart village initiatives
  • Document management and digital archiving practice

AI & emerging technology

Following developments in artificial intelligence and encouraging its responsible use for education, research, innovation and social development — with our own Commission for Digital Ethics and AI reviewing what we build and recommend.

  • AI literacy for teachers, students and public servants
  • Ethical review and human oversight in AI projects
  • Machine learning, data analysis and automation pilots
  • Robotics and STEM demonstration activities

Education & digital skills

Building digital literacy and lifelong learning for students, teachers, academics and communities — through curricula, teaching materials, digital textbooks and e-learning platforms we develop ourselves.

  • Courses, academies, webinars and online training
  • Summer and winter schools, study visits
  • Teacher training and classroom technology support
  • Media literacy and cyber security awareness

Research & academic development

Interdisciplinary research and academic cooperation on digital transformation, artificial intelligence, education and social development — including studies, strategies, methodologies and evaluations for partners.

  • Applied research and analytical reports
  • Policy papers and strategy development
  • Programme and project evaluation
  • Academic publishing and conference organisation

Youth & innovation

Getting young people into technological, educational, scientific and entrepreneurial work early — with formats where they build something real rather than sit through a lecture.

  • Hackathons, competitions and olympiads
  • Innovation forums and youth conferences
  • Start-up and digital entrepreneurship support
  • Volunteering and youth mobility

Digital inclusion

Equal access to knowledge, technology and digital opportunity — with particular attention to rural areas, women, older people, people with disabilities and communities that public programmes usually reach last.

  • Community digital centres and outreach training
  • Accessible learning materials and assistive tools
  • Basic e-services literacy for older citizens
  • Digitisation of cultural heritage and digital tourism
