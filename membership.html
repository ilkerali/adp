Statute, Article 32

Nine categories of membership.

Pick the one that fits your situation — the application is the same, and the Managing Board decides on admission.

Regular members

Adults who submit an application, accept the mission and respect the statute and code of ethics. Full voting rights in the Assembly.

Youth members

Ages 15 to 29. Full access to programmes, projects and working bodies.

Student members

Students enrolled at a higher education institution, at any level of study.

Expert members

Professors, teachers, researchers, engineers, lawyers, economists, ICT experts and consultants. May be appointed to commissions and advisory bodies.

Institutional members

Companies, institutions and organisations, participating through an authorised representative.

International members

Individuals and organisations based outside North Macedonia who work in the same fields.

Volunteers

People who contribute time to events, training and project delivery without a formal employment relationship.

Honorary members

Awarded for outstanding contribution to the association's fields of work.

Founders

Those who took the decision to establish ADP. Equal rights with regular members; the status stays permanently on record.

No applicant may be discriminated against on grounds of nationality, ethnicity, language, religion, sex, age, political conviction, disability, social status or any other ground established by law.

What members do

Membership is participation, not a card.

  • 01Take part in projects and working groups
  • 02Contribute to research and publications
  • 03Attend training, conferences and professional events
  • 04Propose and develop new initiatives
  • 05Vote in the Assembly and stand for the boards
  • 06Connect with national and international partners

Obligations

Members respect the statute and the code of ethics, act in line with the association's principles, protect its reputation and property, and declare conflicts of interest. Membership can be suspended or terminated by decision of the Managing Board following the procedure in the statute.

Membership card

Admitted members receive an ADP membership and identification card. Design, issuing, validity and withdrawal are governed by document ADP-09 in the registry.

See ADP-09

Applying

Four steps.

Applications are reviewed by the Managing Board at its regular sessions. You will hear back either way.

Apply for membership
  1. Step 1

    Send the application

    Write to info@adp.mk with your name, the membership category you are applying for, and a short note on what you would like to work on.

  2. Step 2

    Accept the rules

    Confirm that you accept the statute and the code of ethics. Both are published in the document registry.

  3. Step 3

    Board decision

    The Managing Board decides on admission and records it in the membership register.

  4. Step 4

    Welcome and first assignment

    You receive your membership card and an invitation to the working group or project closest to your field.

Academics, teachers, students, engineers, entrepreneurs and volunteers are all welcome.

Tell us what you can bring and what you want to learn. Both matter.
