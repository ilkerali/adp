Statute, Chapter V

Bodies of the association.

Assembly

Highest body

Made up of all regular members, each with one vote; institutional members take part through an authorised representative. It adopts and amends the statute, approves strategy, elects and dismisses the President and the members of the Managing and Supervisory Boards, adopts annual and financial reports, sets the membership fee and decides on joining national and international networks.

Meets at least once a year In person, online or hybrid Two-thirds majority for statute changes

Managing Board

Executive body · 5–11 members

Elected by the Assembly for a four-year mandate, with re-election possible. Selection takes account of gender equality, professional diversity, breadth of expertise, and integrity.

It carries out Assembly policy, adopts annual operating plans and internal rules, manages projects and property, admits new members, appoints project teams and proposes the budget.

President

Legal representative

Elected by the Assembly for four years. Represents ADP before national and international institutions, signs agreements, chairs the Managing Board and proposes the Secretary General.

Vice-President

Deputy

Stands in for the President when they are absent or unable to act. Specific responsibilities are set by decision of the Managing Board.

Secretary General

Executive management

Professional head of administration: organises daily work, coordinates project delivery, manages documentation and organises financial and administrative reporting.

Supervisory Board

Three members

Elected by the Assembly and never at the same time members of the Managing Board. It monitors the legality of operations, supervises finances and the use of funds, reports to the Assembly and proposes improvements.

Scientific & Advisory Council

Professors, scientists, researchers and engineers advising on academic quality and research direction.

Council for Projects & International Cooperation

Prepares and coordinates project applications, consortia and cross-border cooperation.

Commission for Digital Ethics & AI

Reviews the ethical dimension of technology use, data handling and AI in ADP activities.

The Managing Board

Seven members, one four-year mandate.

Procedure

How decisions are taken.

Meetings of the Assembly, the Managing Board, the Supervisory Board and the other bodies may be held in person, online or in hybrid form, and voting can be carried out electronically.

Ordinary decisions

Majority of the members present, unless the statute or the law requires otherwise.

Statute & status changes

Two-thirds majority of the members present. The same threshold applies to dissolving the association.

Extraordinary sessions

May be requested by the President, the Managing Board, the Supervisory Board, or at least one third of regular members.

Electronic records

Documents signed with a qualified electronic signature or seal count as official documents of the association and are kept in the central digital archive.

Working bodies

The Managing Board may propose standing or temporary commissions, working groups and expert bodies; the Assembly or the Board decides on their establishment.
